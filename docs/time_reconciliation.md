\# Time Reconciliation



\## Principle



Source year, observation period, and analytical comparison period are not assumed to be equivalent.



\## BNIA Vital Signs



BNIA observations retain the source year supplied by the annual Vital Signs workbook.



The year identifies the source's annual record. Individual indicators may require additional methodological review before being interpreted as measurements covering exactly that calendar year.



\## Census ACS



Census observations retain:



\- year: source/API vintage

\- dataset: Census product

\- variable: original Census variable identifier



The current Census population observation is stored as:



\- year: 2024

\- dataset: acs5

\- variable: B01003\_001E



The Census year is not automatically treated as directly equivalent to a BNIA annual observation year.



\## Comparison Rule



Cross-source temporal comparisons must verify compatible reference periods before values are treated as contemporaneous.



If reference periods are not equivalent, the analysis must preserve and disclose that difference rather than silently aligning records by year.
